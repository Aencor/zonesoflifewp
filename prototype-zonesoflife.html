The method

Four Zones, and what each one costs you

This is the vocabulary the whole ecosystem runs on. Knowing which Zone you are in is what makes every other decision obvious.

Red

Out of flow. You react to what happens rather than directing it. Effort produces little.

Amber

Awake. You can name what is not working, but change still depends on how you feel that day.

Green

In flow. Results come consistently because structure holds them, not willpower.

Golden Magic

Mastery. You operate in your Zone and you can take others there.

Privacy policy

Last updated: to be confirmed on publication.

This page is a working outline. Final wording must be reviewed by counsel before launch — the sections below set out what has to be covered, not the finished text.

Who we are

Advanced Coaching & Leadership Center, Inc.. 1400 Camp Letoli Road, Saint Jo, Texas 76265, United States.

What we collect

Information you give us directly: name, email address, telephone number where provided, preferred language, and the interests you select. Information collected automatically: device and browser data, pages visited and referral source, through the cookies described in the cookie policy.

Why we use it

To send you what you asked for, to deliver what you buy or enrol in, to answer enquiries, to measure how the sites are used, and — where you consented — to send the weekly newsletter. We do not sell personal data.

Who we share it with

Service providers processing data on our behalf: host, email platform, payment processor, analytics and course platform. Each is bound to process only on our instructions. A complete processor list must be published here.

How long we keep it

Subscription records for as long as you remain subscribed plus a defined retention period; order and financial records for the period required by law. Exact periods to be set with counsel.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. The GDPR covers EU residents, the CCPA covers California, and the LFPDPPP covers Mexico.

International transfers

We operate from the United States. Data from elsewhere is transferred to and processed there under appropriate safeguards.
