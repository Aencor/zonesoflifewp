Zones of Life: financial funnel and follow-up copy

Version 2.1 · September 24, 2026. From the Financial Mini Profile (free) to the private interpretation session and the core program. Everything is automated up to the session with the coach, which is the first human contact.

Variables between brackets, like {{nombre}}, are CRM fields (kept in Spanish so both language versions read the same database fields). The Have Ability result texts reflect Alan C. Walter's exact foundational teachings.

What changed in this version

v2.1 · Pricing

The bundle with session and the session sold as an upgrade cost the same: $1,640 MXN / $85 USD. Automated profile: $500 MXN / $25 USD.

v2.1 · The Six Abilities

Produce, Focus, Investigate, Have, Invest, and Create Wealth. Named in R1, B5, and B10 reply, feeding {{habilidad_baja}}.

v2.1 · Rescheduling Window

A lead who no-shows has 1 month to reschedule. E1, E2, E3, and E4 state this using {{fecha_limite_reagenda}}.

v2.1 · Workbook

The English and Spanish files are processed separately; D1 and DS1 include the workbook as an attachment.

v2 · Free Product

The old "four zones map" is replaced by the Financial Mini Profile, which measures the Have Ability. Personalization shifts from {{zona_critica}} to {{nivel_tener}} (three levels) during the free stage and to {{habilidad_baja}} (the lowest of the six) after purchase.

v2 · Two Paid Products in Initial Offer

Automated Financial Health Profile ({{precio_perfil}}) and Profile + 60-minute Private Interpretation Session ({{precio_perfil_sesion}}). The automated profile is always the primary CTA; the bundle with session is the secondary option.

v2 · Coach Session Included Early

The coach session appears in the initial offer within the {{precio_perfil_sesion}} package. It is never sold alone or before the profile, but no longer waits until the report is generated.

v2 · Session is Now Paid

Flow D sells the session as an upgrade ({{precio_mejora_sesion}}) to those who purchased the automated tier. Those who bought the bundle enter branch DS, which simply prompts them to schedule.

v2 · Real Deliverables in All Copy

6-abilities chart, Alan C. Walter audio lesson, and the "Taking Charge of Your Financial Future" workbook. Unfulfilled promises (subzone breakdown, cross-zone reading, 90-day plan) removed.

v2 · No False Urgency or Artificial Bonuses

B8 presents the session option instead of a bonus email; B9 is removed; D5 drops artificial seat limits.

v2 · B6 Educational Focus

Replaces hypothetical case studies with educational copy explaining Positive vs. Negative Processes using clear foundational principles.

v2 · New Flow Codes

R1 features three distinct level text variants; P1 splits into P1a and P1b; branch DS1–DS4 added.

System rules

No humans before the coach

Every message prior to the session is automated and signed "Zones of Life Team", never with the coach's individual name.

Questions answered by buttons or AI

Predefined quick-reply buttons and an AI assistant clearly identified as such for open-ended queries.

Scoring changes sequence, not channel

Two or more clicks on the offer or checkout visit activates the accelerated follow-up version.

Automated is the primary CTA

In R1 and Flow B, the main button is always the Financial Health Profile. The bundle with session appears as a secondary choice, never alone.

Meta-approved WhatsApp templates

Outside the 24-hour customer service window, only Meta-approved templates with verified opt-in are used.

Tags from minute one

{{nivel_tener}}, profile, and stage are tagged on Mini Profile completion; {{paquete}} and {{habilidad_baja}} on payment and full profile completion.

YOU HAVE THE ABILITY TO HAVE. Congratulations!
